<objective>
Fix audit #3 and #5. Today `_determine_files_to_delete` treats "tracked but absent from `current_files`" as deleted. `current_files` also omits files whose MD5 failed and files matched by ignore rules, so those get moved to `_trash/` (gone from S3 after 30 days) while still on disk. Separately, `soft_delete_object` on an already-missing S3 object returns False forever, pinning the entry and eating the `max_deletions_per_scan` budget.
</objective>
